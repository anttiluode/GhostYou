# Ghost You

A Halloween webcam toy. Sit still and you are solid; move and you fade until the room shows
through you. Your motion plays a sine-wave theremin: higher on screen is a higher note, more
motion is louder.

Press **Summon the ghost** and allow the camera. For the best effect, press **Learn empty room**
and step out of view for 3 seconds. Everything runs in your browser; no video leaves your machine.
